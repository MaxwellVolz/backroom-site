Backroom

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Backroom: Poker with Friends ("Backroom" or "the app") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Backroom is built and run by Maxwell Thomas Volz, an individual developer. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean the developer.

If you have questions, email us at maxwellvolz@gmail.com.

About the app

Backroom is a social poker app for iOS. It uses play chips only. Play chips have no cash value. You cannot buy them and you cannot cash them out. The app offers no real-money gambling and no prizes. Backroom is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

Signing in

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. When you do, Apple gives us an account identifier and an email address. If you choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

Information we store

We store the following information, and it is linked to your account:

  • Account details: your account ID and the email address Apple provides.
  • Profile: your username (3 to 16 characters) and the avatar you choose.
  • Social connections: clubs you join, your friendships, invites, and referrals.
  • Messages: chat messages you send at tables and in clubs.
  • Player notes: private notes you write about other players. Only you can see these.
  • Reports: reports you submit about other players, including the reason and any details you add.
  • Gameplay: hand histories and gameplay statistics.
  • Presence: whether you are online and which table you are at.
  • Settings: your responsible-gaming settings, such as session reminders and limits, and your other app preferences.
  • Cosmetics: cosmetic items you own and which ones you have equipped.
  • Push notification tokens: stored only if you turn on notifications.
  • Purchase records: records of cosmetic or subscription purchases, which we receive from RevenueCat, the service that processes our in-app purchases. Apple handles payment. We never see your card or other payment details.

Security and game integrity

To prevent fraud and collusion, we log integrity events. These logs may include a device identifier that the app generates and the IP address of your connection. We use this information to protect fair play and the security of the service.

How we use your information

  • create and run your account;
  • run games, clubs, chat, and other social features;
  • show your profile and stats to other players;
  • enforce your responsible-gaming settings and apply your preferences;
  • deliver the cosmetic items and subscriptions you purchase;
  • send push notifications, if you turn them on;
  • review reports, moderate abuse, and keep games fair and secure.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or financial account data.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run Backroom. They handle data only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase: hosted database and authentication. Your account and the data listed above are stored here.
  • Fly.io: hosting for our real-time game server.
  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase processing and validation.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service, and payments.

What other players can see

  • your username and avatar;
  • your actions at the table;
  • chat messages you send;
  • stats shown on your profile.

Your private player notes and the reports you submit are not shown to other players. You can mute and block other players and report abuse from within the app.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists.

Hand histories, ledger entries, and moderation records are kept as permanent records that cannot be changed. We keep them so we can protect game integrity and verify that games were fair.

When you delete your account, it is deleted right away. We remove or de-identify your personal details: your email address, username, avatar, push tokens, friends, player notes, settings, and the text of your club messages. Play chips in your account are forfeited (they have no cash value). We keep de-identified game records (hand histories and the chip ledger), purchase records, and moderation records, including the text of any message that was reported, so we can keep games fair and handle reports.

Your choices and requests

You can delete your account from Settings in the app, or ask us to see or delete your data by emailing maxwellvolz@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

  • turn push notifications on or off in your device settings;
  • change your username, avatar, and preferences in the app;
  • set session reminders and limits in your responsible-gaming settings;
  • mute, block, or report other players.

Rights for users in California, the EEA, and the UK

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access: ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction: ask us to fix information that is wrong.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your personal information, as described above.
  • Objection: object to certain ways we use your information.

To use any of these rights, email maxwellvolz@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. We do not sell your personal information.

Where data is processed

Your information is processed in the United States. If you use Backroom from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed there.

Children

Backroom is not directed to anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
