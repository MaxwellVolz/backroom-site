Support

Need help with Backroom? Email maxwellvolz@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. Including your username and what you were doing when the problem happened helps a lot.

Common questions

Can I buy chips or cash them out?

No. Backroom uses play chips only. They have no cash value, can't be purchased, and can't be redeemed for money or prizes.

How do I invite friends to my club?

Open your club and tap Invite. Share the link; anyone who opens it can join after signing in.

How do I report or block a player?

Tap the player's seat at the table, or long-press one of their chat messages, then choose Mute, Block, or Report.

How do I set session reminders or limits?

Open your Profile and go to the responsible-gaming settings. Changes that loosen a limit take effect after a waiting period.

How do I check that a hand was dealt fairly?

Every deck is committed before the hand starts. Open the hand in your history to replay it; the fairness panel verifies every card.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings (the ≡ button on the home screen), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you. See the Privacy Policy for what is removed and what is kept.

Restoring purchases

Cosmetic purchases and passes are tied to your Apple ID. Use Restore Purchases in the Shop if something is missing.
